Higher Ground: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 (your rights, data security, keeping and deleting data)

This policy covers the Android game Higher Ground (package com.highergroundgames.runner), made by Higher Ground Games (the developer, and the "data controller" under data-protection laws such as the GDPR; contact details at the end). In short: we do not collect, store on any server or sell your personal data. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads (see "Ads" below).

What stays on your phone

Your progress (scores, leaves, diamonds, outfits, upgrades and settings) and the name you choose for co-op races are saved only on your own device. Uninstalling the game deletes them. We have no copy.

Co-op races on your local network

Co-op uses your local Wi-Fi network or a phone's hotspot to connect phones in the same place. While you are in a room, your phone exchanges game data directly with the other phones in that room: your chosen name, outfit, position in the race and score. This data never leaves the local network, is not sent to us or to any server, and is not kept after the race. Only choose a name you are happy for the people in your room to see.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for an in-game reward (getting back up after a crash, a free prize box, part of a big play-money loss back) and, now and then, a full-screen ad between runs, never during one. To show, limit and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address (used for approximate location), ad interactions and crash and performance data. This is described in Google's How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads). Ads are limited to content rated for mature audiences and are never directed at children.

In-app purchases

You can buy packs of diamonds, an in-game currency, and "No Ads" (no ads between runs), through Google Play. Google Play handles the whole payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we never see your card, bank or Google account details. The game only receives the product you bought and a purchase token from Google Play, which it keeps on your phone so a pack is added once. Google Play also tells the game whether you own No Ads, so it comes back on a new phone. Diamonds and everything bought with them stay in the game: they can't be cashed out, sold, traded or turned back into money.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a few reminders on your phone itself (for example when your rent box is full or the daily deals change). They are made and kept on your device; nothing is sent to us or to a server to send them. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

The game engine

Higher Ground is made with the Unity engine. When the game first starts, the engine may send Unity Technologies basic technical facts about the device, such as its model, Android version, graphics chip and screen size, to help Unity make the engine work on more phones. This does not include your name, contacts, files or anything you do in the game. See Unity's privacy policy for players.

No accounts

The game has no accounts, and we use no analytics tools of our own. The casino and stock-market mini-games use in-game play money only: nothing in them can be won for real money or cashed out.

Permissions

The game uses Android's network permissions (internet access, Wi-Fi state and multicast) to find and talk to other phones on your local network for co-op races and to load ads, the advertising ID permission for ads, Google Play's billing permission for diamond packs and No Ads, notifications for the reminders above, and vibration for haptic feedback (which you can turn off in SETTINGS).

Google Play

When you download the game, Google Play handles the download and may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google beyond the anonymous, aggregated statistics Google Play provides to all developers.

How data is kept safe

We hold no personal data on any server, so there is nothing of yours for us to lose. Data that Google's ads and billing services collect is sent encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) and protected under Google's security practices. Your game progress stays in the game's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Game progress and settings are kept on your phone until you delete them. To delete them, uninstall the game, or open Android Settings > Apps > Higher Ground > Storage > Clear storage.
  • Co-op race data is not kept after the race ends.
  • Ads data is kept by Google for the periods in Google's retention policy. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads), and manage data Google holds at myaccount.google.com.
  • Purchases are recorded by Google Play under its own terms; we keep no purchase records.

Because we hold no data about you, there is no account or record for us to delete. If you still want to ask, contact us (below) and we will reply within 30 days.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Personalised ads are shown only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time from SETTINGS > PRIVACY > AD CHOICES. Non-personalised ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests (keeping the game free and the ads honest). Purchases are processed to fulfil the contract you make when you buy. Google may process ads data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in Google's data transfer frameworks.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and other US state laws, Brazil's LGPD, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or limit its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. We do not sell personal data for money. The Google Mobile Ads SDK does share device data with Google for advertising, which some US state laws count as "sharing" for targeted advertising; you can opt out by turning off personalised ads or deleting your advertising ID in your phone's Settings. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights. To use a right that concerns data held by Google, use the Google links above; for anything else, or for a grievance under India's DPDP Act, contact us (below).

Children

Higher Ground is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. If you believe a child has used the game, the steps above delete everything kept on the phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
